Scoring rubric — V2.0

Rubric V2.0: 40 points. This revision changes criterion weights and must not be used to regrade the historical V1 score without labeling a new retrospective analysis. Numerical tolerance is 1% for rounded-input estimates. Equivalent mathematically and biologically defensible approaches receive credit. No pass threshold or validated difficulty level is defined.

 | Q8 | 3 | Corrected observations (1); conditional synthesis/clearance interpretation (1); unresolved mechanism, absent uncertainty and a consequential assumption (1).

Award each listed unit only when both conclusion and supporting reasoning are correct; a multi-point unit may receive integer partial credit for independently correct components. Record the supporting response excerpt and justification for each award. Within the same criterion, an unresolved contradiction or unsupported numerical claim invalidates the affected reasoning credit even if a correct value appears elsewhere. Do not double-penalize the same error across unrelated criteria. Record additional unsupported claims in a separate error log; claims outside any criterion do not create ad hoc deductions. This exposes rubric coverage limits rather than concealing them. Word count is guidance, not a scoring criterion.

For disputed criteria, preserve the initial score and record the alternative interpretation and adjudication. Future evaluators should agree on scoring rules before seeing model identities. No independent adjudication has been performed for this revision.
