# Scoring rubric: 40 points

The rubric scores scientific reasoning, not exact wording. It has not been calibrated using independent reviewers or an AI response dataset.

## Consistent grading rules

Accept approximately 0.1733, 0.1155, 0.0578, 0.0289 and 0.0866 per hour and equivalent algebra. Allow a 1% numerical tolerance for rounding. Credit correct later reasoning that consistently carries a minor earlier arithmetic error, but do not restore points for the original wrong number. Do not double-penalize the same error within a criterion.

For experimental proposals, accept alternatives that measure the required quantity, specify controls and distinguish outcomes. A promoter reporter cannot, on its own, demonstrate direct binding of compound X. A quantitative pathway claim needs evidence beyond protein accumulation.

Do not invent a pass threshold, label scores as Turing-approved, or claim graduate-level difficulty has been measured. Claiming statistical significance earns no credit for the relevant uncertainty criterion; it does not automatically erase unrelated correct reasoning.
